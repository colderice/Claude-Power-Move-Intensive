DEMO PART A — Content Layer (~4 min)
Say This
"Every Monday morning, I sit down for about 15 minutes. I give Claude one topic — something real, something current. Then I run this."
Type Live — Content Batch Prompt
I need this week's content batch. Topic: [current relevant topic — e.g., "How to use Claude Projects to save hours every week"] From this, give me: - 3 LinkedIn/Facebook posts (different angles: insight, story, challenge) - 1 email subject line with 3 variations - 1 short-form hook for Instagram/X (under 80 words) My voice profile is loaded. Write punchy. Direct. No fluff. Real-business energy.
After Output
"That's a Monday morning. Fifteen minutes. Week of content done. I didn't write a single word. I directed it."
DEMO PART B — Follow-Up Layer (~4 min)
Say This
"Layer 3 is what saves the most money. Let me show you a real scenario from my pipeline."
Type Live — Follow-Up Prompt
I had a call last week with a potential consulting client. $5K/month retainer. They were interested, asked good questions, said they'd talk to their partner and get back to me. Five days of silence. Write me a 2-touch follow-up: - Touch 1: Today. Warm. Not pushy. Short. One ask: a yes or no. - Touch 2: Four days from now. Add one specific value point about what they said on the call. Give them an easy out if the timing isn't right. Write it in my voice. Under 80 words each. No corporate filler.
After Output
Read message one out loud. "You'd send that. That's not AI copy — that's how I'd actually say it. That's what Layer 1 does. Everything runs through your voice."
DEMO PART C — Operations Layer (~4 min)
Say This

I have a client call tomorrow. They run a mid-size home services company. Revenue around $2M. Struggling with follow-up and repeat business. They watched my webinar series. Give me: - 3 questions I should ask to understand where their biggest revenue leak is - 2 things I should find out about their current tech stack before recommending anything - The #1 insight about home services businesses and AI that I should be ready to share
After Output
"That replaced 30 minutes of prep. I show up sharp because Claude did the thinking. That's Layer 4."

If Things Go Sideways
Running long → Cut Demo Part C. Parts A and B tell the complete story.
Output sounds off → "Watch what happens when I sharpen this prompt." The redirect IS the lesson.
Tech issue → Have all three prompts pre-typed somewhere to copy. Output + commentary teaches, not the live typing.
