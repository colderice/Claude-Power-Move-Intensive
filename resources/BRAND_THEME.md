# Claude Power Move — Brand Theme

**Project:** The Claude Power Move Webinar Series
**Brand Owner:** John Lawson / ColderICE Media
**Tagline:** People + AI = Power Moves

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Page background | Deep Charcoal | `#141413` |
| Card / container surface | Elevated Charcoal | `#1c1b1a` |
| Primary accent | Burnt Orange | `#d97757` |
| Secondary accent | Steel Blue | `#6a9bcc` |
| Primary text | Warm Off-White | `#faf9f5` |
| Muted / secondary text | Warm Gray | `#b0aea5` |
| Dividers / borders | Dark Border | `#2e2d2b` |

**Usage rules:**
- Burnt orange (`#d97757`) is the power color — CTAs, accent bars, highlighted text, numbered callouts, and left-border callout boxes
- Steel blue (`#6a9bcc`) is used for section labels and subheadings only — never for CTAs
- Off-white (`#faf9f5`) is the primary body text color against dark backgrounds
- Warm gray (`#b0aea5`) is for secondary info: taglines, PS lines, unsubscribe, captions
- Dark border (`#2e2d2b`) for all dividers, card borders, and rule lines
- Background is always warm-dark — never pure black, never white

---

## Typography

| Role | Font | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines / UI | Poppins | 600, 700, 900 |
| Body copy | Lora (serif) | 400, 600 |
| Fallbacks | Arial, Georgia, serif | — |

**Usage rules:**
- Poppins for all display text: headlines, subheadings, labels, buttons, nav, numbered items
- Lora for all long-form reading: email body paragraphs, prose content
- Letter-spacing: 2–2.5px for uppercase labels; 1px for secondary metadata lines
- All section labels are uppercase, small (11–13px), tracked out

---

## Layout & Structure

**Container width:** 600px max (email), flexible for web
**Border radius:** 6px on cards and containers; 4px on buttons

**Structural patterns:**
- Top accent bar — 5px burnt orange strip across the top of every card/email
- Left-border callout box — `border-left: 4px solid #d97757` on dark background for featured content
- Numbered item rows — burnt orange bold number + separator space + body text, with 1px bottom border between rows
- CTA block — orange header bar with dark text, card body with headline + button below it

**Spacing rhythm:**
- Section padding: 36–40px horizontal, 24–36px vertical
- Paragraph spacing: 22px bottom margin
- Divider margin: 28px above and below

---

## Buttons / CTAs

- Background: `#d97757` (burnt orange)
- Text: `#141413` (near-black) — dark text on orange, not white
- Font: Poppins 700
- Padding: 14px vertical / 32px horizontal
- Border radius: 4px
- No border, no shadow

---

## Voice & Copywriting Style

- Short sentences. Punchy. No wind-up.
- Lead with the point.
- No corporate filler ("It's worth noting," "Certainly," "Straightforward")
- Numbered items over bullet lists where possible
- P.S. lines are used for tension or preview — never throwaway
- Preheader text should be a one-line hook, not a description

---

## Logo / Wordmark Treatment

- Brand name appears in two weights: **The Claude Power Move** (light/regular) + **Power Move** (bold, orange)
- "Power Move" is always the visual anchor — heavier weight, often in accent color
- Secondary line: "With John Lawson · ColderICE Media" in muted gray, uppercase, tracked

---

## Social Image Style

- Dark background (matches email palette)
- Bold white headline with "Power Move" in burnt orange
- Editorial photo of John Lawson (no background, or dark seamless)
- QR code appears on all social graphics linking to ClaudePowerMove.com
- Footer strip: "Power Moves · Claude · You" or similar lockup in small caps
- Session number and host name appear at top in small uppercase orange/white
