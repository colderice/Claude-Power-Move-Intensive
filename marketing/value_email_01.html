The 5-minute Claude setup most people skip.

Here's why most people don't get real results from Claude.

They open it like a search engine. They type a question. They get a generic answer. They shrug and go back to doing things the old way.

The problem isn't Claude. The problem is that Claude doesn't know who you are, what you do, or how you talk. Every conversation starts from zero.

There's a fix. It takes 5 minutes. Almost nobody does it.

The Power Move

Write Claude a short brief about your business — who you are, who you serve, what you sell, and how you like to communicate. Paste it at the top of every conversation. That's it.

Here's a simple template to get you started:

Your Business Brief (copy + customize)

You are working with [YOUR NAME], [YOUR TITLE] at [YOUR BUSINESS NAME].

We serve [DESCRIBE YOUR AUDIENCE].

Our main offer is [WHAT YOU SELL OR DO].

My communication style is [DESCRIBE YOUR VOICE — direct, warm, professional, etc.].

Always write in my voice. Keep things concise and action-oriented.

What changes when you do this

01   Claude writes in your voice instead of sounding like a robot
02   Outputs are specific to your business, not generic filler
03   You stop rewriting everything and start actually using what Claude gives you

Five minutes of setup. Dramatically better results. Every single session.
