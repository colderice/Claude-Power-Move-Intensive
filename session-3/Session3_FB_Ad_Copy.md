## SCROLL-STOPPING CREATIVE CONCEPTS

The existing series graphic (Claude Power Move 1x1.png) works as a fallback — but it doesn't stop anyone mid-scroll. These concepts are built specifically for Session 3's follow-up theme. Each one is designed to hit the pain before the person reads a single word of copy.

---

### CONCEPT A — "The Read Receipt" *(Recommended for Version 1)*

**What it is:** A mock email thread on a dark brand background. Styled to look like a real inbox. Shows a sent proposal email — subject line: *"Proposal: 90-Day Marketing Strategy Engagement"* — and below it in muted gray: *"Delivered · 14 days ago · No reply."*

**Why it stops the scroll:** Every business owner has had this exact moment. They see it and feel it before they even register they're looking at an ad. The pain recognition happens in under a second.

**Production:** Build in Canva or Genspark. Dark background (#141413). Use system-font styling to mimic an email client. Keep it simple — one sent message, one status line. No clutter.

**Pair with:** Version 1 copy. The visual and the headline ("The money is in the follow-up.") hit the same nerve.

---

### CONCEPT B — "Text as Creative" *(Recommended for Version 3 / mobile)*

**What it is:** No photo. No illustration. Just words on the brand dark background.

Line 1 (white, Poppins 700, large): *"They didn't say no."*
Line 2 (burnt orange #d97757, Poppins 900, large): *"They just went quiet."*

Small lockup at bottom: Claude Power Move logo + ClaudePowerMove.com in muted gray.

**Why it stops the scroll:** Text-only creatives look different from everything else in the feed. People pause to read before they realize they're reading an ad. Two-line structure creates a micro-beat — first line lands, second line twists.

**Production:** 5-minute build in Canva or Genspark. 1080x1080 for feed, 1080x1920 for stories. Don't add anything else — the restraint is the design.

**Pair with:** Version 3 (short punch). Also test against Version 1 with the Read Receipt — whichever CTR wins, that's your cold audience creative.

---

### CONCEPT C — "John + The Line" *(Best for Version 2 / warm audience)*

**What it is:** Editorial photo of John — dark seamless background, straight-on or slight three-quarter angle. Bold text overlaid:

*"You don't have a lead problem."*
*(pause line)*
*"You have a follow-up problem."*

Burnt orange accent bar across the top. URL at the bottom in small uppercase.

**Why it stops the scroll:** John's face builds trust with warm audiences who've seen him before. The line is designed to reframe their belief about why their pipeline is stuck — that reframe creates pattern interrupt.

**Production:** Source from existing editorial photos or record a clean still frame from the cam script video. Drop into Canva. Brand dark background (#141413). Text overlay in Poppins 700 white + orange. Keep the composition clean — face takes 60% of the frame, text takes 40%.

**Pair with:** Version 2 (demo-forward). The face + the reframe gets the warm audience to click. The body copy does the rest.

---

### CONCEPT D — "15-Second Video Clip" *(Best overall performer for mobile)*

**What it is:** A direct-to-camera clip cut from the 3-minute cam script. First 3 seconds are the hook — delivered straight to camera, no wind-up:

*"You don't have a lead problem. You have a follow-up problem."*

Then 10–12 more seconds: "Right now you've got proposals that never got a response. Leads that went quiet. Past clients you keep meaning to reach out to. This Thursday at 7pm, we fix that." Hard cut. Register link on screen.

**Why it stops the scroll:** Face-to-camera video is the highest-trust format in the feed. John's delivery is direct and credible — it doesn't look produced, it looks like someone who actually knows what they're talking about.

**Production:** Shoot in one take. Dark or neutral background. No graphics overlay needed for the ad itself — just clean captions. Square (1:1) for feed, vertical (9:16) for stories and Reels. Export a version with captions burned in — 85% of mobile video plays on mute.

**Pair with:** Version 3 (short punch) body copy as the caption. Or run with no caption text — let the video carry it and use a one-liner: "This Thursday. Free. ClaudePowerMove.com."

---

### CONCEPT E — "The Leaky Pipeline" *(Optional / awareness play)*

**What it is:** Simple illustrated graphic. Left side: icons representing leads/proposals flowing in. Center: a funnel with a hole in it labeled "Follow-Up" with money icons leaking out. Right side: Claude logo plugging the hole. Arrow pointing right to a calendar: *"Thursday, May 7 · 7PM ET."*

**Why it stops the scroll:** It makes the abstract problem visual in a single frame. No one has to read anything — they see the problem and the fix immediately.

**Production:** Build in Genspark (AI-generated illustration) or Canva. Keep colors on-brand — dark background, orange and blue accents. Don't over-design it. The simpler the illustration, the better it reads at small sizes.

**Pair with:** Version 1 or Version 2. Good for LinkedIn where slightly more polished graphics perform well.
