## THE 4-STEP PROMPT STACK

---

### STEP 1 — THE MONDAY BRIEF

**What you say:**
"First thing I do Monday morning — I don't open email. I open Claude. I tell it what's on my mind for the week. And it helps me shape the idea before I build anything."

**Type this live:**
```
Here's what's on my mind for content this week.

Topic: Why most business owners stay invisible online even when they're posting every day.

My take: it's not about posting more. It's about posting with a point of view. Most people are creating content but not communicating anything. There's a difference between being active and being present.

Before we build anything — sharpen that idea. Give me the core point of view in 2-3 sentences. The version that cuts through.
```

**What you should see:**
A sharp, opinionated core idea. Not a list of tips. Something you'd actually say on a Facebook Live.

**What to say after:**
"I'm not generating yet. I'm deciding. Is this the right angle? Does this land? Now I approve it and we build. Watch."

---

### STEP 2 — SOCIAL POSTS

**What you say:**
"Five posts. Different hooks, same core idea. And here's the key — I'm not writing these. I'm reviewing them. Claude generates. I decide which ones go out."

**Type this live:**
```
Good. Let's build from that.

Write 5 Facebook posts from this core idea. Each one needs a different hook — a different way into the same point.

Match my voice. Lead with the hook. No wind-up. Under 150 words each. End with a question or CTA that earns engagement.
```

**What you should see:**
Five distinct posts. Five different openings. All landing on the same idea. Sounds like John, not an agency.

**What to say after:**
"I'm not writing. I'm reading. Which two go out this week? Which one do I tweak? Which one do I save? That's the shift. Creator to editor. Drop in the chat — which one would you post?"

*(Read 2–3 out loud. React naturally. If one needs fixing, fix it live — that's the editing lesson.)*
